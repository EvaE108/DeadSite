# Puzzle-Site-Mess-Around

This is my little passion project. I was inspired by some online escape rooms like Notpron to create
a series of online puzzles. I also love horror so I made it a horror themed one. Note to solve this you will need a laptop and almost definitely the internet. Hopefully you enjoy! (:

Puzzle Solution Guide (spoilers ahead)

1) To continue press ctrl U on the keyboard and follow the instructions from the comments at the bottom. The answer here is article
2) This one is just the riddle on the piece of paper. The answer is death
3) For this one you need to use the highlighter to find the hidden word underneath the riddle text. The answer is counting
4) For this one you must recognize that each line is a movie quote from 4 different movies that have numbers in the title: Seven, Super 8, The Sixth Sense, and Thirteen Ghosts. You then take that number letter from the quote. The final answer is need.
5) This one is a bit more like a video game. You just have to traverse the maze fast enough in the alloted time to find all the letters. When you unscramble them you get Runner. Then you should notice the title of the page is Maze. This gives you Maze Runner. Then in the comments in the source code it asks who is he. If you look up who the maze runner is you should find his name is Thomas which is the answer.
6) This one is pretty tough. For this one each box is a letter. You need to figure out that the reference to **keys** is atually refering to keys on a computer. To determine the starting key you need to figure out which musical note is being played at the start for each box (this is when the box glows red). The starting notes are F,G,A,D,A, and G. From there you need to figure out how to traverse the keyboard. If the note moves up in pitch (like from C to D), you should move a step to the right on the keyboard. If the pitch moves down you should move a step to the left. If the volume increases you should move up a step on the keyboard and if the volume goes down you should move a step down. After every traversal you should get the answer scream.
7) This one is purely for my own enjoyment, so apologies in advance he he he. Basically I put together 4 of my favorite movie jumpscares. During the moment of the jumpscare in each clip a letter will briefly flash onto the screen. Once you get all 4 letters it should spell the answer which is trap.
8) This is my puzzle puzzle. To do this one you solve the puzzle. Then you should use the internet to recognize the image is from The Walking Dead, specifically you should look up what the sign in the photo is referencing, this will lead you to the answer which is terminus.
9) This one is my most evil puzzle he he he. For this one, once you type in terminus you will see a page that looks like the page you get if the answer is incorrect, however upon further inspection you can see that this page is a fake and the answer is wheel.
10) That's the end! 🥳🥳🥳
